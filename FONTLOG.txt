FONTLOG for Trica Neon
======================

This file provides detailed information on the Trica Neon font software. This information should be distributed along with the Trica Neon fonts and any derivative works.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Trica Neon is a geometric typeface generated from centerline skeletons stroked at a single width. Wide letters fit a square module, outer corners are rounded and inner joints stay sharp.
