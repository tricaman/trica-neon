FONTLOG for Trica Neon
======================

This file provides detailed information on the Trica Neon font software. This information should be distributed along with the Trica Neon fonts and any derivative works.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Trica Neon is a geometric display typeface drawn with a single, even stroke, made for headlines, logos, signs and interfaces, and for short texts such as captions and labels. The design grows from one module, a pill as wide as the capital height and half as tall: every curve is half or a quarter of that pill, outer corners are rounded and inner joints stay sharp. The glyphs are generated from centerline skeletons stroked at the width of each weight, so all weights share one construction.


ChangeLog
---------

(This should list both major and minor changes, most recent first.)

Version 1.000 (not released yet)
- First version: six weights from Thin to SemiBold, as static fonts and as a variable font with a weight axis.
- GF Latin Core character set, with combining marks positioned by anchors.
- Class kerning, and the ccmp, locl (Romanian and Moldovan), frac, numr, dnom, sups, ordn, tnum and case features.
- Vertical metrics: a 1.3 em line with the capitals centred in it.
