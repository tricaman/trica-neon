Trica Neon is a geometric display typeface drawn with a single, even stroke, like a line of neon tube. The design grows from one module: a pill as wide as the capital height and half as tall, with fully round ends. Every curve is half or a quarter of that pill, so one family of radii runs through the whole alphabet. Outer corners are rounded and inner joints stay sharp, which gives the letters soft outlines and crisp junctions.

It is made for headlines, titles and logos, for posters, signs and interfaces, and it feels at home in short texts such as captions, labels and menus. For long reading, it pairs well with a quieter text face.

Wide capitals fill a square, lowercase ascenders reach the capital height and the generous spacing keeps the strokes apart in every weight. The family comes in six weights, from a hairline Thin to SemiBold, also as a variable font, and covers the GF Latin Core character set with kerning, mark positioning, fractions, superiors, ordinals and tabular figures.

To contribute, see github.com/tricaman/trica-neon.
